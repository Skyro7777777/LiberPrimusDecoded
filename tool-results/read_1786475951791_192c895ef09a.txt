   320→
   321→This is the single strongest piece of structural evidence in the entire Kasiski dataset:
   322→- The probability of a random 5-gram occurring twice in 12,956 runes at any specific distance is roughly 12,950 / 29⁵ ≈ 0.019 (i.e. about 1.9%).
   323→- The probability of the second occurrence happening at *exactly* distance 1031 (one of the three parable factors) is roughly 1/12950 × 0.019 ≈ 1.5 × 10⁻⁶ — about one in 680,000.
   324→- This is a striking numerical coincidence, strongly suggesting Cicada deliberately placed OUNWM at this distance to signal that **1031 is structurally important to the cipher** — possibly:
   325→  - as the actual autokey primer length (1031 runes long, derived from the parable text),
   326→  - as a key-stream period,
   327→  - as a seed for a prime-index recurrence that generates the key stream.

   383→
   384→1. **The autokey cryptanalytic signature is real and reproducible** — our toolkit's frequency analysis exactly matches CicadaSolvers' published numbers (12,956 runes; 0.6638% doublet rate; 5.19× suppression factor; IC normalized = 0.9999; DJUBEI occurs exactly 2 times; OUNWM repeats at distance exactly 1031).

   389→   > "LICE THE INSTAR TVNNELNG TO THE SVRFACE / WE MVST SHED OVR OWN CIRCVMFERENCES / FIND THE DIVINITY WITHIN AND EMERGE"
   390→   >
   391→   > Gematria sums of the three lines: 1259, 1031, 1229 (all prime) — product = 1,595,277,641.
   392→

Its distance factorisation (5 × 1279) gives candidate key lengths of 5, 1279, and 6395. 5 is small enough to be a classical Vigenère key length but doesn't match any of our 20 keys' lengths; 1279 is prime.
   396→
   397→5. **Pure Vigenère is refuted**: even with F-skip discovery, the score range tells us no key in our 20-candidate list works as a pure Vigenère key. Autokey is the live variant.
