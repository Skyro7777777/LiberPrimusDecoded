    26→## Step 1 — The Lag-5 Paired-Coincidence Framework
    27→
    28→### 1.1 What it is
    29→
    30→The **lag-5 paired-coincidence test** is a *fourth-order* statistical test on a ciphertext stream. It was discovered by the `aldegonde` cryptanalysis toolkit in June 2026 and documented in `docs/lag5-phenomenon.md`.
    31→
    32→Given the rune stream C[0..N−1] (12,956 runes for the 55 unsolved LP2 pages), define the lag-5 match indicator:
    33→
    34→```
    35→M[i] = 1  iff  C[i] == C[i+5]    (for i = 0 .. N-5)
    36→```
    37→
    38→A **d1-event** is a pair (M[i]=1, M[i+1]=1) — the digraph C[i..i+1] repeats verbatim 5 positions later, i.e. the pattern `X Y · · · X Y`.
    39→
    40→A **d4-event** is a pair (M[i]=1, M[i+4]=1) — consecutive 5-grams agree in their first and last runes, i.e. the pattern `A · · · B A · · · B`.
    41→
    42→### 1.2 The observation in LP
    43→
    44→| Statistic | Observed | Uniform expectation | z (local) |
    45→|---|---|---|---|
    46→| Σ M (mono lag-5 matches) | 479 | 446.6 | +1.5σ (unremarkable) |
    47→| pairs at separation d=1 | **29** | 15.4 | +3.5σ |
    48→| pairs at separation d=2 | 15 | 15.4 | — |
    49→| pairs at separation d=3 | 14 | 15.4 | — |
    50→| pairs at separation d=4 | **28** | 15.4 | +3.2σ |
    51→| pairs at separation d=5 | 19 | 15.4 | — |
    52→| separations 6–25 | all flat | 15.4 | — |
    53→
    54→Joint statistic T = d1 + d4 = 57 vs null 30.7 ± 6.5 → local z ≈ +4.1. Family-blind Monte Carlo over the (lag × separation) grid gives **p ≈ 0.033** — the fairest significance figure. Wide dragnet over 399 4-point templates dilutes this (p ~ 0.43), as any p≈0.03 effect dilutes.
    55→
    56→### 1.3 Why a decade of analysis missed it
    57→
    58→The anomaly is a 4th-order (4-point) correlation. Standard tests — IoC, kappa, Friedman, bigram tables, mutual information, autocorrelation — are all 1st/2nd-order. The lag-5 anomaly's *entire* 2nd-order footprint is the +1.5σ mono kappa at lag 5, which is indistinguishable from noise (≈ 38 against a noise floor of ±41 in the full 29×29 contingency χ²).
    59→
    60→### 1.4 The d1/d4 crib-drag mechanism
    61→
    62→The 57 events (29 d1 + 28 d4) are **deterministic ciphertext copies** — a copy carries no fresh information, so the plaintext at the copied positions must come from somewhere. Three interpretations are information-theoretically possible (per `hypotheses/lag5-back-reference.md`):
    63→
    64→| # | Interpretation | Implication |
    65→|---|---|---|
    66→| (a) | **Nulls** — the copied runes are NULLS, the decryptor skips them | The 57 events are skip-markers; remove 208 positions from the cipher stream |
    67→| (b) | **Coincidence** — the keystream repeated AND the plaintext repeated by chance | Rate-disfavored: requires ~18% key-pair reuse, would push mono kappa-5 to ~1.14 (observed 1.073) |
    68→| (c) | **Back-references** — the events fire only where the plaintext repeats at lag 5 ("repeat the previous digraph/frame") | Gives ~114 plaintext crib equations `P[i] = P[i-5]` for any key-search attack |
    69→
    70→### 1.5 Zodiac-340 precedent
    71→
    72→The Zodiac-340 cipher (cracked Dec 2020 by an international team after 51 years) was solved starting from this exact statistical family: repeated two-symbol sequences at trial offsets, peaking at lag 19. The cracking recipe:
    73→
    74→1. Identify the high-order coincidence pattern (exactly what aldegonde found for LP).
    75→2. Hypothesize the cipher is **transposition + substitution** (not pure substitution).
    76→3. Test transposition patterns (diagonal reads, columnar reads, etc.).
    77→4. Crib-drag with common phrases.
    78→
    79→**Caveat from `lag5-phenomenon.md` §5:** aldegonde verified the Z-340 *mechanism class* (homophones + transposition) is excluded for LP, because that class leaks at 2nd order at this sample size, and LP does not. So the Zodiac-340 transposition attack is unlikely to work, but worth a definitive test (Step 4 below).
    80→

   187→
   188→For period-5 Vigenère (the lag-5 hypothesis), the global rune offset mod 5 determines which coset the crib falls in:
   189→
   190→| Phase | Crib | Cipher rune | Implied add-kv | Implied bea-kv |
   191→|---|---|---|---|---|
   192→| 0 | (none) | — | — | — |

   311→## Step 6 — Custom Lag-5 Paired-Coincidence Attack
   312→
   313→### 6.1 Approach
   314→
   315→From scratch: extracted all d1 and d4 events from the unsolved corpus (12,956 runes), used them as plaintext constraints under interpretation (c) (back-references: P[i] = P[i-5]).
   316→
   317→For each lag-5 event position, under the back-reference hypothesis:
   318→- Plaintext satisfies P[i] = P[i-5]
   319→- Under additive cipher: key[i] - key[i-5] = (cipher[i] - cipher[i-5]) mod 29
   320→- This gives a KEY-RELATION constraint (not a key-value constraint)
   321→
   322→If the cipher were period-5 additive (key[i] = key[i-5] for all i), all such constraints would have key_diff = 0.
   323→
   324→### 6.2 Results
   325→

   333→  key_diff = 0 (rune ᚠ = F)  count = 114 (100%)

   407→**PARTIALLY, BUT NO USABLE KEY.** The 4 cribs give ~28 bits of known-plaintext constraint, but they don't chain (per `contraction-cribs.md` Prediction 2). 144 (key, phase) combinations matched 1-3 cribs locally, but no key matched all 4 cribs simultaneously at any phase. The cribs are local filters, not key-recovery levers.

   410→**NO.** 2.4M tests across 7 transpositions × 8 cipher widths × 12 cribs × 2 methods × 8 pages returned 159 hits with ≥3 char match (top: 5/7). Zero full-match (7/7) hits. The Zodiac-340 transposition+substitution class is FALSIFIED for LP — confirming aldegonde's note that this class would leak at 2nd order at this sample size, and LP does not.

   413→**NO.** 16,240 (page, N, key) tests across 17 Cicada-significant N-values and 20 keys. Top trigram score (page 55, N=5, INSTAR) is z=+6.46 vs random null, but the plaintext is gibberish. Statistical elevation does not equal decryption. Combined with aldegonde's mathematical theorem refuting all additive keystream ciphers (doublet floor 1.7% vs LP's 0.66%), the entire additive cipher family is FALSIFIED.

   415→### Any breakthrough?
   416→**NO.** The unsolved LP remains unbroken. The lag-5 anomaly is real, replicated, and mechanistically diagnostic, but:
   417→
   418→1. **It is NOT a period-5 polyalphabetic signal** (coset IOC test flat at z<+1.8 for all rules).
