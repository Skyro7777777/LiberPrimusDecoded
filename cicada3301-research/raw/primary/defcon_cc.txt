 Join a fascinating conference talk from DEF CON 31 where four community leaders from CicadaSolvers share their nine-year journey attempting to crack one of the most challenging cryptographic puzzles ever created - the Cicada 3301 Liber Primus. Dive into the cultural impact of this mysterious book of elder futhark runes and codes that has stumped cryptographers since 2014. Learn about the various decryption strategies, technical approaches, and collaborative efforts employed by the community in their persistent quest to solve this enigma. Gain valuable insights into the future of collaborative puzzle-solving and understand how the Liber Primus continues to challenge modern cryptography. Whether interested in cryptography, puzzle-solving, internet mysteries, or the power of community persistence, discover the compelling story behind this ongoing cryptographic challenge that requires no prior technical knowledge to appreciate.
