Features

 Converts locally in your browser between Latin text and the Gematria Primus rune alphabet.

 Starts in Auto mode and detects whether the input should be encoded from Latin text or decoded from Gematria Primus runes.

 Supports both directions with a single mode control and updates the input/output labels automatically.

 Applies Liber Primus-style aliases such as U/V, C/K, S/Z, ING, and IO/IA.

 Decodes ᛝ as (I)NG and ᛡ as I(O/A) so ambiguous readings remain visible.

 Treats unsupported characters as word separators, then normalizes repeated separators to one space.

History

 Gematria Primus appeared during the Cicada 3301 puzzle cycle in 2013. The alphabet assigns 29 runes to
 English letter values and also gives each rune a decimal position and a prime-number value. It became a central
 part of Liber Primus, where pages are written with these runes rather than plain Latin letters.

Where It Is Used

 Transcribing Cicada 3301 Liber Primus pages into Latin-readable values.

 Comparing solved and unsolved Liber Primus passages while preserving rune ambiguity.

 Computing Gematria Primus sums from prime values or decimal rune values.

 Working with modulo-29 cipher experiments and other Liber Primus analysis workflows.
