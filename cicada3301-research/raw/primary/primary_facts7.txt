What is the meaning of the skull in Escher's eye

Escher's skull in the eye reminds us of mortality. He is teasing us by reflecting his version of "Death" back at us from the pupil of his eye. His "Death" is the one who watches over us all, the fate of each of us. In Escher's narrative there is no great expanse of life for us at all, just the small time that we have, constantly being watched by "Death", just drawing nearer to the inevitable conclusion of our destinies. In fact, Escher is an "illusion" artist, so the picture is therefore an illusion - As one looks into the eye, one sees right through to their skull, which is obviously impossible thereby creating the illusion.

I believe the eye photo may be a hint towards the hidden numbers and Rasputin images on onion 6.
